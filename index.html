About

Software engineer since 2006, building mobile apps since 2007. I started with Windows Mobile in C# and have worked almost entirely on iOS for years, mostly on apps for retail and insurance customers.

I'm curious about how things work and I like making them work. The best part of the job is taking an idea and turning it into something a person can hold: designing a feeling into an interaction, packing an experience into an app, using logic in a way that feels a bit like art.

More on my professional work is on LinkedIn.
